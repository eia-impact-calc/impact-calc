Bars use a log scale so small and large impacts stay comparable. The background shows the Low, Medium and High zones; lines mark H = 25 and H = 200.

How it works

  1. Add a pollutant or impact.
  2. For each criterion A–G, tap the description that fits.
  3. The app multiplies the seven scores to get H and classifies it.
  4. Use the biggest-lever tip to choose where mitigation helps most.

Classification

H < 25 · LowAcceptable. Keep routine monitoring.
25 – 200 · MediumControl and/or mitigation measures warranted.
H > 200 · HighPriority impact. Mitigation required.

H = 25 and H = 200 sit on the band edges; the app counts both as Medium.
